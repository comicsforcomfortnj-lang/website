The mission

It’s a pretty simple idea.

I’m Holden Wood. I’ve got a big comic collection, and people keep donating more, so I started Comics for Comfort to get all of it to folks who could use a good story.

Every comic gets bagged, boarded, sorted, and checked for the right age group before it goes anywhere. Then we drop it off around town or ship it to hospital charities and troops overseas, and we write down every delivery. We do this every week all summer, not just once.

The whole point is comfort. Hand someone a good comic when they could use one.

How we treat every book

  • Always bagged, boarded, and standing up in real comic boxes. Never dumped in loose.
  • Checked by title and label so nothing ends up on the wrong shelf.
  • We photograph the books, boxes, and crew. No faces or inside-the-building shots without written permission.
How it works

Here’s how it goes.

STEP 01

Collect & stage

Comics come out of the collection, already bagged and boarded, and go into labeled boxes. We count as we go.

STEP 02

Sort & screen

Every book gets a color: green, yellow, or red. That’s how we make sure nothing ends up somewhere it shouldn’t. Anything we’re not sure about gets a second look.

Green · all-ages Yellow · review Red · adults only
STEP 03

Deliver & log

Then we hand them off to local spots or ship them to hospital charities and troops overseas. Every drop gets written down and photographed.
