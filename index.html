Why we started

I am Holden Wood, and I started Comics for Comfort in the summer of 2026. I always read comics with my dad when I was a kid, and I thought there were many others out there who'd appreciate a similar experience.

Every issue we receive is bagged, boarded, sorted, and screened for the right age group before it goes anywhere. We hand deliver around town, ship to hospital charities and deployed units, and record every single delivery. We run a session every week through the summer.

How it works

How every session runs

SORT!
STEP 01

Collect and stage

Comics come out of storage, bagged and boarded, and go into labelled boxes. We count as we go.

CHECK!
STEP 02

Sort and screen

Every comic gets a colour: green, yellow, or red. Nothing moves until it has one.

DELIVER!
STEP 03

Deliver and log

Boxes go to clubs, libraries, hospitals, and units overseas. We write down every delivery.

Screening

Nobody has to guess

Every book lands in one of three boxes. An adult clears the yellow and the red before anything leaves the table.

GreenSafe for kids

Goes to clubs, libraries, and hospitals.

YellowNot sure

An adult checks it before it goes anywhere.

RedAdults only

Never goes in a box meant for kids.
